Cat & Frog

🐱 Cat

A D walk · S poop · W green poop

Score when a poop is left long enough to stink (green: +2). Green poop makes the frog dizzy for 1 second if it eats it. One every 5 seconds.

🐸 Frog

← → hop · ↑ jump · ↓ tongue · / spit

Fetch poop: 1 point from the grass, 2 if caught in the air. Spit up at the cat: a hit makes the cat dizzy for 1 second.

Two players on one keyboard. Space starts a 60-second round.

This game needs a keyboard.
